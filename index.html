Esports in Seoul

How gaming spaces, team culture, and esports history can turn competitive gaming into a physical culture in Korea.

Gaming experience

Gen.G Gaming Xperience

Gen.G Gaming Xperience is a PC bang branded under the famous Korean esports team Gen.G. GGX combines the traditional PC bang experience with professional team branding all in one location.

  • Gaming Experience
    • I purchased a few hours of playtime and experienced using the equipment provided at the PC bang. The high-end setup made competitive gaming feel accessible without requiring visitors to bring their own equipment.
  • Food and Convenience
    • Food and drinks could be ordered directly to your seat while playing, allowing visitors to stay at their PC without having to get up at all.
  • Language and Accessibility
    • Most of the PC interface was in Korean, which made some parts of the experience more difficult as a foreign visitor, but the overall process was still manageable.
  • Team Identity
    • The Gen.G branding and team merchandise store connected the PC bang experience with professional esports, which made this location feel different compared to a typical PC bang.
Team Space

Base camp

T1 Base Camp

T1 Base Camp is a team-branded PC bang and fan space connected to the esports team T1, which is one of if not the most popular esports organizations in the world, not just Korea. The location combines gaming, merchandise, food, and team branding, creating a space where visitors can interact with T1 beyond simply watching matches online.

  • Team Identity
    • T1 branding was much more noticeable throughout the space, making the location feel closely connected to the organization rather than like a normal PC bang.
  • Fandom and Merchandise
    • Merchandise and team-related displays gave fans a way to interact with T1 physically instead of only through broadcasts or social media.
  • Space Popularity
    • During my visit, I noticed that the space itself was often very busy, showing that the location functioned as both an active gaming space and as a tourist/fan destination.
  • Gaming and Fandom Together
    • T1 Base Camp combined the normal PC bang experience with professional esports branding, allowing visitors to participate in gaming culture while also engaging with a specific team.
