More than just the game

Esports in Seoul

How gaming spaces, team culture, live competition, and esports history turn competitive gaming into a physical culture in Korea.

Gaming experience

Gen.G Gaming Xperience

Gen.G Gaming Xperience is a PC bang branded under the famous Korean esports team Gen.G. GGX combines the traditional PC bang experience with professional team branding all in one location.

  • Gaming Experience
    • I purchased a few hours of playtime and experienced using the equipment provided at the PC bang. The high-end setup made competitive gaming feel accessible without requiring visitors to bring their own equipment.
  • Food and Convenience
    • Food and drinks could be ordered directly to your seat while playing, allowing visitors to stay at their PC without having to get up at all.
  • Language and Accessibility
    • Most of the PC interface was in Korean, which made some parts of the experience more difficult as a foreign visitor, but the overall process was still manageable.
  • Team Identity
    • The Gen.G branding and team merchandise store connected the PC bang experience with professional esports, which made this location feel different compared to a typical PC bang.

Base camp

T1 Base Camp

T1 Base Camp is a team-branded PC bang and fan space connected to the esports team T1, which is one of if not the most popular esports organizations in the world, not just Korea. The location combines gaming, merchandise, food, and team branding, creating a space where visitors can interact with T1 beyond simply watching matches online.

  • Team Identity
    • T1 branding was much more noticeable throughout the space, making the location feel closely connected to the organization rather than like a normal PC bang.
  • Fandom and Merchandise
    • Merchandise and team-related displays gave fans a way to interact with T1 physically instead of only through broadcasts or social media.
  • Space Popularity
    • During my visit, I noticed that the space itself was often very busy, showing that the location functioned as both an active gaming space and as a tourist/fan destination.
  • Gaming and Fandom Together
    • T1 Base Camp combined the normal PC bang experience with professional esports branding, allowing visitors to participate in gaming culture while also engaging with a specific team.

Live game

OWCS Live Game

Live esports makes competition feel more intense being in person in comparison to virtual. The screen becomes a stage, the crowd shares moments andreacts together, and the match becomes something people can attend for an entertaining time.

  • Live Spectator Experience
    • Attending this OWCS event changed the idea of esports being something I usually view from a screen at home into a physical event. Everyone at the venue was focused on the same stage, players, and moments during the match all at the same time.
  • Crowd Participation
    • Crowd participation is probably the biggest aspect of a live esports event. The audience becomes a part of the experience, as fans will often cheer before, during, and after matches together. Even though the game itself was still played on a screen, the live audience created a similar atmosphere to other live sports events.
  • Event Production
    • The large screen, lighting, player booths, commentary, match introductions, transformed the gameplay into something more than just that. The production made the players and teams feel like professional competetors rather than people simply playing a game.
  • Esports Culture Overall
    • Despite my very limited experience with overwatch, the live environment made the competition easy to become invested in. Just like other sporting events I was able to follow teams, players, and crowd reactions without a deep understanding of the game itself.

History

Esports Hall of Fame

The Esports Hall of Fame presents esports as history. It gives star players, top performing teams, and impactful tournaments a public place where people can remember them for the rest of time.

  • Building History
    • The exhibits presented Korean esports through a timeline rather than focusing only on current teams and games. One of the earliest sections connected the growth of esports with the rise of PC bangs and broadband internet in the late 1990s.
  • Player Legacy
    • Jerseys, player displays, awards, and other artifacts treated professional players as people whose careers were worth preserving. Seeing these objects displayed behind glass felt very different from seeing current esports merchandise in T1 Base Camp or GGX.
  • Recognition Beyond the Game
    • The Hall of Fame also showed how esports has developed beyond individual games. Games and teams change over time, but the museum preserves players, organizations, tournaments, and major developments as part of a larger esports history.
  • Connection to PC Bangs
    • One of the most interesting parts was seeing PC bangs discussed historically after having already visited modern team-branded PC bangs myself. GGX and T1 Base Camp showed what these spaces look like today, while the Hall of Fame helped explain why PC bangs became important to Korean gaming culture in the first place.
